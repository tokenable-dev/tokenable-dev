FAQs

Answers for both sellers and buyers.

What is a vault?

A way to hold your card so you can sell or trade it instantly without shipping anything each time. The real card stays safely stored; only ownership trades. With PSA Vault, PSA stores it; with Partner vault, a contracted partner stores it.

What’s the difference between PSA Vault and Partner vault?

PSA Vault — PSA verifies your card, stores it, insures it while stored, and ships it when someone redeems. Partner vault — a contracted partner stores it, attests to it, and ships it on redeem; it isn’t independently PSA-verified or insured by the platform. Every listing shows which vault a card is in.

Can I choose Partner vault?

Partner vault is only for contracted partners. If you’re an individual seller, your card goes through PSA Vault — it’s verified, insured while stored, and shipped for you.

Is my card insured?

Cards in PSA Vault are insured while stored. Partner vault cards are not insured by the platform.

Should I trust a Partner vault listing when buying?

Partner vault cards are held and attested by a contracted partner, but they’re not independently PSA-verified or platform-insured. If you want independent verification and insured storage, choose a PSA Vault listing. The badge on every listing tells you which is which.

Are there fees?

Storing a card is free. Fees apply when someone redeems — that covers the withdrawal and shipping. You’ll see the exact cost before confirming, with no markup.

How do I get the physical card?

Redeem it: pick the card(s), enter your address, review the cost, confirm and pay. For PSA Vault, PSA ships it from the vault; for Partner vault, the partner ships it. Once it arrives it shows as in your possession.

Can I sell a card without ever shipping it?

Yes — that’s the point. When it sells, ownership transfers instantly and the card stays in its vault. It only ships when someone redeems.

Who verifies the card is real?

For PSA Vault, PSA grades and verifies it before listing. For Partner vault, the contracted partner attests to it; it isn’t independently PSA-verified. The badge tells you which.
