FAQ

Answers for both sellers and buyers.

What is a vault?

A vault is where a card sits while it is listed and traded. Because the card stays in one place, it can be bought and sold without being shipped each time. When you want the physical card, you redeem it.

What is the difference between PSA Vault and Partner vault?

With PSA Vault the card is sent to PSA, checked against its certification, and stored and insured there. With Partner vault the owner keeps the card. We do not hold it, do not verify it and do not insure it.

Which one should I choose?

Choose PSA Vault if you want the card verified and stored before it goes live, and you can wait for shipping and intake. Choose Partner vault if you want to list today and you are comfortable standing behind the card yourself.

Is my card insured?

Cards are insured while they are stored at PSA Vault. Partner vault cards are not insured by us — they stay in the owner's hands and under the owner's own arrangements.

If I sell a Partner vault card, what am I responsible for?

Everything about the card. You confirm it is authentic, that the grade and cert number are correct, and that its condition matches your listing. You also ship it to the buyer when the sale requires it. If the card is not as described, that is on you, not on us.

Should I trust a Partner vault listing when buying?

Judge it on its own. A Partner vault card has not been checked by us or by PSA at listing time, and it is not held by us. The trust is between you and the seller. If you want a card that was verified before listing, look for the PSA Vault badge.

Are there fees?

Yes. Listing and selling carry a platform fee, and PSA Vault adds storage and intake costs. Redeeming a card has a shipping and handling cost, charged at what it costs — with no markup. The exact amounts are shown before you confirm anything.

How do I get the physical card?

Redeem it. You enter a shipping address, review the cost, and confirm. For PSA Vault, PSA ships from the vault. For Partner vault, the owner ships it themselves. See the redemption steps in your portfolio for details.

Can I sell without ever shipping?

If the card is in PSA Vault, yes — it stays in the vault and ownership transfers to the buyer. With Partner vault the card is in your hands, so shipping is your responsibility when the buyer wants it.

Who verifies the card is real?

PSA does, for cards sent to PSA Vault — each card is matched to its certification at intake. For Partner vault cards nobody verifies them on our side; the seller alone stands behind the card.
